    About Pretty Critical

    Hosted by Grace — a beauty and lifestyle influencer, Pretty Critical looks beyond the glossy visuals and empowering slogans to ask a bigger question: when brands speak about feminism, are they challenging gender expectations or turning empowerment into another product to sell?

    Through candid podcast conversations, interactive campaign breakdowns, and community discussions, we make media criticism accessible to everyday consumers.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What exactly is Pretty Critical?

    Pretty Critical is an independent podcast and media platform exploring beauty culture, feminism, and brand communication through conversations, campaign analysis, and audience participation.

    What do you mean by feminist advertising?

    Feminist advertising uses messages about women's equality, independence, empowerment, or challenging gender stereotypes to communicate with audiences.

    Are you against beauty brands or buying beauty products?

    Not at all. We do not automatically support or reject brands. We examine how they communicate feminist ideas and encourage audiences to make their own informed judgments.

    How do you decide whether a campaign is empowering or exploitative?

    We look at representation, visual choices, language, campaign context, and how the feminist message connects to the brand or product. We consider different interpretations rather than assuming every campaign has the same intention.

    Can I participate in the Weekly Verdict?

    Yes! Listen to the episode, explore the campaign, and cast your vote. You can also compare your interpretation with other listeners' perspectives. Go to the Weekly Verdict →
